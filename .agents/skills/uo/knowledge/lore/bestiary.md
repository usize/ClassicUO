# Bestiary

A threat-tier guide to creatures you'll actually run into. This is general UO
knowledge, not this server's exact stat tuning — treat tiers as relative
danger, not guaranteed numbers. Always sanity-check with `./ai/uo mobiles` /
`./ai/uo summary` (HP%, notoriety) before engaging, per `SKILL.md`'s safety
rules. On the map glyph legend: `A` = animal, `M` = hostile creature.

## Trivial / newbie tier

Safe for a fresh, unequipped character. Little to no real threat.

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Rabbit, chicken, squirrel | Harmless animals, sometimes tameable, occasional food/leather |
| Rat, giant rat | Weak melee, common starter target, minor disease risk (giant rat) |
| Sheep, cow, pig | Farm animals — tameable/herdable, resource sources (wool, meat, leather), not aggressive |
| Slime | Very weak, but can be poisonous in some variants — low melee threat |

## Low tier

Manageable for a character with basic combat skill and starter gear.

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Orc, orc scout | Melee, hits harder in groups (orc camps) — mind being surrounded |
| Skeleton | Basic undead melee, resistant to some damage types, no poison risk |
| Headless, ratman | Melee, ratmen sometimes cast weak magic |
| Zombie | Slow, melee, can inflict disease |
| Gazer (small) | Ranged energy attacks — riskier for melee-only characters |
| Ettin | Two-headed giant, hard-hitting melee, still manageable solo with decent gear |

## Moderate tier

Group content or a well-equipped solo character. Expect to actually manage HP.

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Ogre, troll | Heavy melee hitters, trolls sometimes have knockback/rage |
| Ogre lord | Tougher ogre variant, hits harder |
| Lich | Undead spellcaster — poison and energy bolts, keep distance or interrupt casting |
| Earth/air/fire/water elemental | Magic-resistant to their opposing element, solid melee or ranged depending on type |
| Gargoyle (hostile variants) | Flying, can be ranged or melee depending on type |
| Solen (red or black) | Hive-based insectoid, poison risk, dangerous in numbers near their hives |
| Dread spider, giant spider | Poison melee, web/immobilize risk |

## High tier

Farmed by geared groups or strong solo builds; genuinely dangerous to an
undergeared character.

| Creature | Notes |
|---|---|
| Dragon, drake | Heavy melee + fire breath (AoE), high HP, valuable scales/hides as loot |
| Lich Lord | Stronger lich — same caster threat, more HP and damage |
| Daemon | Strong melee + fire magic, common "boss-adjacent" mid-dungeon threat |
| Titan | Very heavy melee hitter, high HP |
| Balron | Powerful demon — melee, teleport, energy bolt, poison; a serious threat even for groups |
| Bone Dragon | Undead dragon variant — melee + magic, sizeable HP pool |

## Named bosses / uniques

Room-boss or dungeon-champion tier. Expect a dedicated group, or a very
strong solo character, and a real plan (escape route, healing, buffs).

| Boss | Where (typically) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Barracoon | Fire dungeon | The Piper — summons/commands rats, ranged+summon threat |
| Semidar | Terathan Keep | Terathan queen, strong melee/poison, guarded by her hive |
| Lady Mel | Painted Caves | Corrupted spirit, magic-heavy |
| The Fan Dancer | Fan Dancer's Dojo (Tokuno) | Bushido/Ninjitsu-flavored fights, martial-arts themed boss |
| Dark Father | Deceit | Strong undead/demonic caster boss |
| Medusa | Various (event/dungeon dependent) | Petrification-flavored fight, high magic damage |
| Mondain | Story/event content | Original central UO villain — see `world-and-history.md` |
| Minax | Story/event content | Mondain's avenger — see `world-and-history.md` |
| Exodus | Exodus Dungeon | Sentient machine-god boss, heavy magic and mechanical minions |

Titans like **Dread Horn** and **Osiredon** exist in later-era content
(Stygian Abyss and beyond) as high-end raid bosses — treat any encounter with
them as end-game difficulty and don't solo one blind.

## What creatures are farmed for

- **Gold** — nearly everything drops some.
- **Reagents** — some casters (liches, mages) drop spell reagents.
- **Hides/scales/feathers** — dragons, drakes, and other large beasts; used in Tailoring/Leatherworking (see `items-and-resources.md`).
- **Special drops** — named bosses and champion spawns can drop power scrolls, artifacts, or unique items; see `items-and-resources.md` for what a power scroll actually is.
