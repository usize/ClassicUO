# Tokuno Islands

Added in the Samurai Empire expansion, Tokuno is a small three-island chain with a
Japanese/samurai theme — the source of the Bushido and Ninjitsu skill lines, and the
crafting materials/recipes tied to them. Its single town, Zento, sits on Makoto-Jima.
See `overview.md` for the coordinate system and `moongates.md` for travel here — all
three islands have their own public moongate.

All coordinates below come from `servers/ModernUO/Distribution/Data/Locations/tokuno.json`.
