# Malas

Added in the Age of Shadows expansion as a refuge after Trinsic's fall to the Blackthorn
invasion. Malas holds two towns and the entrance to **Doom**, one of the game's classic
endgame dungeons. See `overview.md` for the coordinate system and `moongates.md` for how
to travel here (Luna and Umbra both have public moongates).

All coordinates below come from `servers/ModernUO/Distribution/Data/Locations/malas.json`.

## Luna

A walled, guard-heavy mage/vendor city — **no player-vs-player combat or stealing is
permitted inside its walls**, making it one of the safer hubs to shop and bank in.
Popular with high-end player vendors.

## Dungeons

### Doom

A vertical, gauntlet-style dungeon culminating in Semidar and the Lich Lord bosses —
high-risk, high-reward, built for organized groups rather than solo play.

| Area | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entrance | 2367 | 1268 | -85 |
| Tunnel | 2352 | 1267 | -110 |
| Inside | 381 | 133 | 33 |
| Gauntlet | 433 | 331 | -2 |
| Guardian's Room | 365 | 15 | -1 |
| Lamp Room | 470 | 95 | -1 |

Note the large coordinate jump between "Tunnel" and "Inside" — Doom's interior is its
own disconnected map region (a common UO dungeon-instancing trick), so once inside,
its internal coordinates are not spatially near Umbra even though the entrance is.
