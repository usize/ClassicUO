# Felucca

The original Britannia. Non-consensual PvP is on everywhere outside guarded
town centers, and death here means full-loot — anything not blessed/insured
can be taken from your corpse. Guards (`G` on the map) patrol town centers and
will strike down anyone who commits a crime in their sight; wilderness and
dungeons have no such protection. Felucca shares its geography with
`trammel.md` — the same city sits at the same X/Y on both facets — but they
are different physical maps: you cannot `goto` between them, only travel via
`moongates.md` or a recall/gate spell.

See `overview.md` for the coordinate system and general travel method, and
`moongates.md` for the fixed public-gate network (Moonglow, Britain, Jhelom,
Yew, Minoc, Trinsic, Skara Brae, Magincia, Buccaneer's Den on this facet).

All coordinates below are sourced verbatim from this server's
`Data/Locations/felucca.json`.

## Dungeons

Entrance coordinates get you to the door; sub-levels are separate teleport/stair
destinations reachable only from inside — treat the deeper Z values as
"where you'll land once you're already in," not places you can `goto` directly
from the surface.

### Covetous
Multi-level dungeon known for ratmen, and the Corpser deeper in. Entrance (2499, 919, 0) · Level 1 (5456, 1863, 0) · Level 2 (5614, 1997, 0) · Level 3 (5579, 1924, 0) · Lake Cave (5467, 1805, 7) · Torture Chambers (5552, 1807, 0).

### Deceit
Undead-heavy dungeon (skeletons, liches) with a reputation for traps. Entrance (4111, 432, 5) · Level 1 (5188, 638, 0) · Level 2 (5305, 533, 2) · Level 3 (5137, 650, 5) · Level 4 (5306, 652, 2).

### Despise
Orc- and ettin-infested dungeon, historically home to an orc chief encounter deep inside. Entrance (1298, 1080, 0) · Entryway (5587, 631, 30) · Level 1 (5501, 570, 59) · Level 2 (5519, 673, 20) · Level 3 (5407, 859, 45).

### Destard
Home to Destard's dragons and drakes — a classic mid-to-high level dungeon, not for new characters. Entrance (1176, 2637, 0) · Level 1 (5243, 1006, 0) · Level 2 (5143, 801, 4) · Level 3 (5137, 986, 5).

### Hythloth
Deep, difficult dungeon with demons and a long history as an end-game farming spot. Entrance (4721, 3822, 0) · Level 1 (5905, 20, 46) · Level 2 (5976, 169, 0) · Level 3 (6083, 145, -20) · Level 4 (6059, 89, 24).

### Shame
Elemental- and spellcaster-heavy dungeon; notoriously mazy layout. Entrance (514, 1561, 0) · Level 1 (5395, 126, 0) · Level 2 (5515, 11, 5) · Level 3 (5514, 148, 25) · Level 4 (5875, 20, -5).

### Wrong
Ratmen and spider-themed dungeon near Wind. Entrance (2043, 238, 10) · Level 1 (5825, 630, 0) · Level 2 (5690, 569, 25) · Level 3 (5703, 639, 0).

### Terathan Keep
Terathan (giant ant-like humanoid) stronghold with a champion spawn room. Entrance (5451, 3143, -60) · Level 1 (5342, 1601, 0) · Champion Room (5205, 1585, 0) · Starroom (5139, 1767, 0).

### Fire Dungeon
Fire elementals and salamanders; two surface entrances. Entrance (5760, 2908, 15) · Brit Entrance (2923, 3407, 8) · Level 1 (5790, 1416, 40) · Level 2 (5702, 1316, 1).

### Ice Dungeon
Frost creatures and an ice demon lair; two surface entrances. Entrance (5210, 2322, 30) · Brit Entrance (1999, 81, 4) · Level 1 (5875, 150, 15) · Ratman Room (5834, 327, 18) · Ice Demon Lair (5700, 305, 0).

### Orc Cave
Straightforward orc-clearing dungeon, good for lower-level combat training. Entrance (1019, 1431, 0) · Level 1 (5137, 2014, 0) · Level 2 (5332, 1376, 0) · Level 3 (5272, 2036, 0).

### Painted Caves
Small single-entrance cave system. Entrance (1716, 2993, 0).

### Palace of Paroxysmus
Boss lair of Paroxysmus, the poison-themed champion-spawn boss. Entrance (5576, 3018, 25).

### Prism of Light
