# Ilshenar

Added in the Lost Britannia Reborn (LBR) expansion, Ilshenar is a separate landmass
south of Britannia — no ship access, reached only by moongate or Recall/Gate Travel.
It has no true "cities" in the Britannia sense; instead it holds scattered settlements
(including a Gargoyle city, since Ilshenar borders the volcanic Gargoyle homeland),
open wilderness, and a dense cluster of dungeons and mines. Its most important feature
is the eight **Virtue moongates** — see `moongates.md` for that table; this file does
not repeat it. See also `overview.md` for the coordinate system and how facet travel works.

All coordinates below come from `servers/ModernUO/Distribution/Data/Locations/ilshenar.json`.

**Dungeon flavor:** Exodus dungeon houses the ancient machine-intelligence boss Exodus.
Blood, Sorcerers, and Spectre are undead/spellcaster-themed multi-level dungeons typical
of Ilshenar's darker tone. Wisp is the largest here (8 sub-levels) — expect a long,
maze-like descent. Treat any Ilshenar dungeon as harder than an equivalent Felucca/Trammel
one; the facet as a whole skews toward higher-level content.
